# Cappers & Code Brand Guide

Version: 1.0 — September 2026

## Brand position

Cappers & Code is a sports-intelligence community built around AI models, human insight, data, strategy, collaboration, and disciplined decision-making. The brand should look like a high-end sports analytics platform—not a generic picks page.

## Core language

- Primary tagline: **AI Models. Human Insight. One Edge.**
- Positioning line: **The Sports Intelligence Community.**
- Supporting line: **More Than Picks.**
- Community line: **Analyze. Model. Discuss. Evolve. Win. Together.**
- Performance line: **Real Data. Real Wins. No Fluff.**
- Trust line: **Locked In. Trust the Code.**
- Campaign line: **The Code Wins Together.**
- Winner line: **Chef Made It.**

## Color system

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core background | Near black | `#050608` |
| Secondary background | Charcoal black | `#0B0E0C` |
| Primary accent | Electric green | `#B6FF00` |
| Deep accent | Signal green | `#79E000` |
| Primary type | Soft white | `#F5F7F2` |
| Muted type | Cool gray | `#9DA59D` |

Electric green is a signal, not wallpaper. Use it for key words, data, outlines, energy, and calls to action. White carries most information. Black establishes premium weight.

## Typography direction

- Headlines: bold condensed athletic display sans; tall, dense, uppercase.
- Energy copy: controlled brush display, uppercase, reserved for one emotional phrase.
- Body/data: clean geometric sans, high legibility.
- Data/HUD labels: compact technical sans or mono-inspired face.
- Never use more than one condensed display, one brush face, and one utility sans in a single layout.

## Logo system

The approved lockup uses a white `CC` monogram framed by electric-green code brackets/chevrons. `CAPPERS & CODE` sits below in spaced all-caps; the ampersand is green.

Rules:

- Keep the mark level, symmetrical, and high contrast.
- Prefer black, near-black, or transparent placement.
- Maintain clear space equal to at least half the monogram height.
- Do not recolor with gradients, distort, skew, outline excessively, or separate the green brackets from the CC without an intentional monogram application.

## Visual world

- Black stadium, tunnel, tactical, or data-lab environments.
- Cinematic electric-green rim lighting, smoke, controlled confetti, sparks, and volumetric atmosphere.
- Restrained sports-data panels, model readouts, grids, charts, and scan lines.
- Premium black metal, carbon fiber, technical fabric, smoked glass, and luminous green interfaces.
- Strong depth hierarchy: one hero message, one character focal point, one proof/data zone, one CTA.

Avoid generic casino chips, dice, slot-machine imagery, rainbow neon, red/gold casino palettes, cartoon illustration, cluttered stock charts, or fake sportsbook interfaces presented as real slips.

## Character architecture

### CC Core Robot

The primary platform mascot: a faceless futuristic armored operator with a glossy black segmented exosuit, luminous green visor, and CC marks on helmet and chest. It represents intelligence, discipline, strength, and system-level analysis.

### Dufflebag Mack Cyber Chef

The winner persona: a masked cyber-chef with a tall black CC toque, glowing CC visor, tailored black chef uniform with green piping, and an optional chef cleaver. It represents a winning pick being "cooked" and should be reserved for winner, recap, and high-conviction creative.

Do not merge the two identities into one costume. Do not show human skin or a recognizable human face. Do not replace CC with another symbol.

## Social composition

- Primary canvas: Instagram portrait, 1080 × 1350 px (4:5).
- Keep critical copy and faces/visors inside the centered safe area.
- Use one dominant headline, typically white with one electric-green phrase.
- Keep the logo in the top zone and CTA/footer in the lower zone.
- When using a sportsbook screenshot, clearly frame it as user-provided proof and never fabricate balances, odds, results, or endorsements.

## Voice

Confident, data-literate, communal, concise, and earned. The brand should create urgency without sounding desperate. Speak as a system and a community—not as a loud tout.

Preferred vocabulary: edge, model, data, strategy, community, locked in, trust the code, level up, intelligence, insight, discipline.

Avoid guaranteed-win language, reckless betting claims, fake scarcity, and unsupported performance percentages.

### Detroit Lions @ Buffalo Bills — Thu, 9/17, 8:15 PM ET, Highmark Stadium

#### Offseason

**Buffalo:** fired Sean McDermott in January, promoted OC Joe Brady to head coach, hired Pete Carmichael as OC (keeps the downfield-passing identity) and Jim Leonhard (ex-Broncos) as DC, who is converting a defense that ranked 30th vs. the run in 2025 (5.14 YPC) from a 4-2-5 into a multiple, attacking 3-4.

- **Detroit:** kept DC Kelvin Sheppard, but he's expanding nickel usage and blending 4-3/3-4 fronts after the defense was exploitable on early downs in 2025.
- **Detroit roster hit:** lost RB David Montgomery to Houston in free agency; his replacement Isiah Pacheco just had back surgery and is out for a significant stretch, and CB Terrion Arnold was released — leaving Jahmyr Gibbs a true workhorse and the secondary thin.
- **Week 1 changed the picture:** Detroit's safety-less secondary (Brian Branch and Kerby Joseph both start the year on PUP) got carved up after halftime by New Orleans (31-30 OT loss allowed); Buffalo's new Leonhard defense gave up 124 rush yards (3.9 YPC) and a big day to ex-Lion Montgomery in a 36-31 shootout win over Houston.

#### Offense vs Defense

**DET offense vs. BUF defense:** Jahmyr Gibbs (29 att/156 yds/2 TD, 5 catches Week 1) is the focal point and profiles as a plus matchup vs. a Bills front still installing Leonhard's 3-4 that just allowed 124 rush yards/3.9 YPC. Amon-Ra St. Brown is Detroit's high-volume red-zone/possession piece (10-of-14, 2 TD) while Jameson Williams remains boom-or-bust downfield (4-of-9, 45 yds) and Sam LaPorta is a steady 6-8 target TE who could find seams if Buffalo's retooled LB corps struggles in coverage the way Detroit's did against Kincaid.

- **BUF offense vs. DET defense:** Josh Allen (20-of-29, 334 yds, 2 pass TD, 2 rush TD Week 1) faces a Lions secondary without both starting safeties and now without Arnold at corner; expect heavy volume again for Dalton Kincaid (130 yds Week 1, most by a Bills TE since 1967) and DJ Moore (100 yds, TD). Detroit's pass rush (5 sacks on Tyler Shough, led by a healthy Aidan Hutchinson) is the counterpunch against a Bills O-line adjusting to a new staff.

#### Injuries

**Detroit (short week):** Questionable — Christian Mahogany (knee/hip; pulled from Week 1, Campbell said "not out of the woods"), Blake Miller (OT, DNP Monday), Juice Scruggs (C, limited) — three O-line starters banged up. Christian Izien (S, groin) was a full practice participant and trends toward playing. Brian Branch (Achilles) and Kerby Joseph (knee) remain on PUP; Cade Mays (wrist) and Payton Turner on IR.

- **Buffalo:** Out — Ty Johnson (hamstring), T.J. Sanders (DT, knee), Tyrell Shavers (WR, ACL), Dorian Strong (CB, NFI). Newly added but expected to play — Cole Bishop (knee/groin) and Terrel Bernard (calf), both finished Week 1. DJ Moore was NOT added to the injury report — clean bill of health entering Thursday.

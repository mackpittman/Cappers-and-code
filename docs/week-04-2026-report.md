# NFL Week 4 2026 — Touchdown Probability & Parlay Report

**Prepared:** Thursday, October 1, 2026.
**Scope:** all 16 Week 4 games, Sun, 10/4, 1:00 PM ET through Sun, 10/4, 1:00 PM ET.
**Method:** one researcher per game, working from the previous week's finals, current injury reports, current market lines and posted anytime-touchdown prices where a book had them up. Win probabilities are vig-removed from the moneyline and sum to one. Implied team points are derived from the spread and the total. Touchdown estimates are the desk's own, built from goal-line share, end-zone targets, implied team total and the defence's touchdown-rate weaknesses — they are not the book's number with the hold stripped.

> Week 4 research prepared Thursday Oct 1 from Week 3 finals, Thursday practice reports and Wednesday/Thursday market lines. ## 4. Game by game

### Arizona Cardinals @ New York Giants — Sun, 10/4, 1:00 PM ET, MetLife Stadium

**ARI -2.5** · total **44.5** · ML -142 / +120 · win prob 56% / 44% · implied ARI 23.5 – NYG 21

#### Offseason

**Arizona cut Kyler Murray loose and rebuilt the backfield around the No. 3 pick.** Murray was released and signed with Minnesota; Jacoby Brissett, 1-11 as a starter last year but a career 3,366 yards and 23 touchdowns in 14 games, kept the job. Notre Dame's Jeremiyah Love (1,372 yards, 18 touchdowns, Doak Walker winner) went third overall, the highest back drafted since Saquon Barkley, and Tyler Allgeier was signed from Atlanta as the veteran complement.
- **The depth behind them is already gone:** James Conner reworked his deal to stay but opened the season on IR (foot), Trey Benson is on IR, and rookie corner Will Johnson went to IR with a neck injury last week.
- **New York is in year one under John Harbaugh,** with Baltimore imports Isaiah Likely and Patrick Ricard and a signed Najee Harris behind Cam Skattebo.
- **The quarterback room has turned over twice in two weeks.** Jaxson Dart had meniscus and MCL surgery after Week 2 and is out for the regular season; Jameis Winston started the last two, and the Giants sent a 2027 fifth-round pick to Minnesota for J.J. McCarthy, Jim Harbaugh's Michigan quarterback, on Monday. Harbaugh reaffirmed Winston as the starter.
- Odell Beckham Jr. was released Tuesday after 20 snaps and zero catches in three games; Brian Burns tore an ACL and went to IR the same day.

#### Offence against defence

**Jameis Winston has not thrown a touchdown as a Giant, and that is the whole game.** He is 25 of 49 for 229 yards, no touchdowns and an interception across two starts, the offense is 0 for 4 in the red zone in that span (6 points at the Rams, 12 on four field goals against Tennessee), and his two best red-zone throws were a pick at the Los Angeles 18 and a minus-1-yard completion at the Tennessee 8. Left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) has not practiced all week.
- **New York has become a run-first team under Winston:** 33 carries for 138 yards last week, Skattebo 20 for 60 and Najee Harris 9 for 52. That meets an Arizona run defense that gave up 6.1 yards a carry to San Francisco and 162 yards on 40 carries to Seattle.
- **Arizona's defense is the leak on the other side.** It has allowed 6.46 yards per play (second most), eight passing touchdowns (third most) and six touchdowns to wide receivers (tied for most). Purdy and Lock combined for 7 touchdowns and 11.0 and 9.0 yards per attempt the last two weeks. Nabers (10 targets in the two Winston games) and Likely are the only Giants with a red-zone target this year.
- **Brissett is throwing a lot, mostly short, mostly to McBride and Wilson.** 82 of 117 for 652 yards, 4 touchdowns, 1 pick; 38 of 52 at San Francisco. McBride has 26 catches on 34 targets, 211 yards, 2 scores, eight red-zone targets with three inside the 5. Michael Wilson has 31 targets (17 last week), 163 yards, a touchdown from the 5 and five red-zone looks. Marvin Harrison Jr. has 9 targets and zero inside the 20.
- **Love is a bell cow now.** 41 carries for 160 yards and 10 catches; 21 carries and 5 catches last week while Allgeier had 2 carries for minus-1. Love has nine of Arizona's eleven red-zone carries by a back and the only one that scored from the 5. Brissett took both carries from the 1 last week and scored on the second; Allgeier's lone goal-line carry (Week 1, from the 3) went for nothing.
- **The Giants' pass rush is a problem for them:** one sack in three games, second fewest, and Burns is gone. New York has allowed touchdowns on 7 of 9 red-zone trips, though it held Tennessee to 1 of 3 and 7 points.
- **Both offenses are bottom-three in yards per play and top-five in possession time,** which means long drives and few of them: Arizona has run 10 or 11 drives a game, New York as few as 7. The Cardinals are 6 of 11 in the red zone.

- **Cardinals:** S Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (back) DNP both days and has not practiced since last Thursday, trending out; C Hjalte Froholdt (knee), DT Roy Lopez (groin) and DT Walter Nolen III (shoulder) limited both days; LB Mack Wilson Sr. (thumb) limited then full. Budda Baker, Isaac Seumalo and Josh Sweat were rest days Wednesday and full Thursday.
- **Cardinals on IR/out:** RB James Conner (foot), RB Trey Benson, CB Will Johnson (neck), G Chase Bisontis, TE Jameson Geers; TE Tip Reiman is out.
- Weather is a non-factor for an outdoor game: 65°F, 8% precipitation chance, gusts to 10 mph.

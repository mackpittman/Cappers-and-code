**Prepared:** Tuesday, September 22, 2026.
**Scope:** all 16 Week 3 games, Thu, 9/24, 8:15 PM ET through Mon, 9/28, 8:15 PM ET.
**Method:** one researcher per game, working from the previous week's finals, current injury reports, current market lines and posted anytime-touchdown prices where a book had them up. Win probabilities are vig-removed from the moneyline and sum to one. Implied team points are derived from the spread and the total. Touchdown estimates are the desk's own, built from goal-line share, end-zone targets, implied team total and the defence's touchdown-rate weaknesses — they are not the book's number with the hold stripped.

### Atlanta Falcons @ Green Bay Packers — Thu, 9/24, 8:15 PM ET, Lambeau Field

**GB -6** · total **44.5** · ML +235 / -290 · win prob 29% / 71% · implied ATL 19.25 – GB 25.25

#### Offseason

**Atlanta rebuilt the top of the building.** Kevin Stefanski replaced Raheem Morris as head coach, Ian Cunningham took over as GM, and Matt Ryan came back as President of Football Operations, with Tommy Rees calling offense and Jeff Ulbrich running the defense. The Falcons added Tua Tagovailoa as insurance behind Michael Penix Jr., drafted CB Avieon Terrell, WR Zachariah Branch and LB Kendal Daniels, and unveiled new uniforms. **Green Bay's offseason was defined by subtraction.** Jonathan Gannon replaced Jeff Hafley as defensive coordinator, center Elgton Jenkins left for Cleveland, and Micah Parsons is still rehabbing the ACL and meniscus he tore last December with a mid-October target. Josh Jacobs has not played a snap, opening the year on the Commissioner's Exempt List after a domestic battery case in Wisconsin, which pushed Green Bay to trade a sixth-rounder to Pittsburgh for Kaleb Johnson on August 30.

#### Offence against defence

**Two of the least efficient offenses in football meet on three days' rest.** Atlanta carries a league-worst -53.1 offensive EPA through two weeks, the worst two-game mark since the 2006 Raiders, a league-high seven giveaways, and the distinction of being the only team that has not run a single red-zone play. Green Bay is not much healthier on that side: 31st in rushing EPA, a league-low 19% third-down conversion rate (4 of 21), and 45.8% of its drives failing to produce a first down. The Packers are throwing at the second-highest rate in the league (66.7%) because the ground game has produced just 129 yards on 39 carries, and Jordan Love's splits are brutal when the pocket breaks - 33.3% completions and a 42.8 rating under pressure against 63.6% and a 118.4 rating when clean. That matters because Atlanta is third in the NFL in pressure rate (43.4%) while blitzing just 18.5% of the time (29th), and it is facing a Packers line that lost RT Zach Bako-Bewele to a torn patellar tendon. **The counterweight is Green Bay's defense**, which is second in yards per play allowed (3.9), holds RBs to a league-low 2.8 yards per carry, and gave up only 3.6 yards per pass play on early downs.

#### Injuries

**Atlanta got its quarterback back and lost its best corner.** Michael Penix Jr. (knee) practiced in full Monday and Tuesday and was named the Week 3 starter, his first start since a partially torn ACL ended his 2025 in November, with Cooper Rush having started the first two losses. A.J. Terrell Jr. went on injured reserve Tuesday with a groin injury and cannot return until Week 7, pushing C.J. Henderson opposite Mike Hughes, while DE Samson Ebukam (hamstring) has not practiced; Tua Tagovailoa (oblique), Brian Robinson Jr. (ankle) and Jawaan Taylor (rib) all upgraded to full. **Green Bay's list is longer and uglier.** Jayden Reed (back, carted off in New York) did not participate either day and RT Zach Bako-Bewele is out for the season with a torn patellar tendon, leaving a line where Aaron Banks (knee/toe) missed Monday and Donovan Jennings (hand) and Anthony Belton (elbow) are limited. Javon Hargrave (knee/concussion) and Edgerrin Cooper (shoulder) were limited both days on defense, while Josh Jacobs (Commissioner's Exempt List) and Micah Parsons (ACL, targeting mid-October) remain unavailable entirely.

#### Odds

**The market has moved toward Atlanta since it opened.** Green Bay opened -7 (and briefly -7.5 at some shops) with a 46.5 total; DraftKings now shows GB -6 with the total bet down to 44.5 and the moneyline at ATL +235 / GB -290. FanDuel is a half-point longer at -6.5 with +220 / -270, and earlier SI-quoted numbers had -7 / -325 / +260, so the steam has been on the Falcons' side and the under. De-vigged, that is roughly a 71% Green Bay win probability and team totals near 25.25 for the Packers and 19.25 for Atlanta. **Model and public opinion are split**: Dimers projects Packers 25, Falcons 17 with a 74.4% GB win probability, while Covers leaned Atlanta +7.5 and NBC's Vaughn Dalzell took the Falcons +6.5, citing Green Bay's line injuries. The under has cashed in both Atlanta games and both teams are 0-2 against the spread.

#### The touchdown board

**The board is priced around one Falcon and three Packers.** BetMGM has Bijan Robinson alone at the top for Atlanta at -165 (62.3%), with Drake London +185 the only other Falcon inside +300, and ESPN BET is a tick sharper at Robinson -160 and Watson +140 while DraftKings has been seen at Robinson -165 and London +195. Green Bay's board is flat and cheap - Christian Watson +125, MarShawn Lloyd +165, Matthew Golden +175, Tucker Kraft +180 and Kaleb Johnson +180 - which is what happens when a team throws on 66.7% of snaps and runs a true three-man backfield committee. **Watson is the only Packer who has actually finished drives**, with three of Green Bay's four passing touchdowns, including two in Week 1 against Minnesota. Tucker Kraft's usage exploded once Reed went down: 98.3% of snaps and an 84.4% route rate in Week 2, and Atlanta plays single-high on 64.6% of snaps, which is the look that feeds seam tight ends. The Falcons side is capped by the fact that they have literally never snapped the ball inside the 20 this year, so every Atlanta number other than Robinson should be treated as a projection, not a trend.

#### Offseason

**Buffalo reset the sideline and got an immediate return.** Sean McDermott was fired after the playoff loss to Denver and Joe Brady was promoted from coordinator to head coach, and the Bills are 2-0 with 36+ points in both games. They added **DJ Moore** by trade from Chicago, **Bradley Chubb** and **C.J. Gardner-Johnson** on defense, and opened the new Highmark Stadium with a 41-31 win over Detroit after a 36-31 win at Houston.
- Josh Allen has 582 pass yards, 5 pass TDs, 4 rush TDs and zero turnovers, and is the **+250 MVP favorite**.
- Buffalo leads the league at 7.1 yards per play and 38.5 points per game, but has allowed 31 points in each game.
**Los Angeles hired the offense it wanted and it has not shown up yet.** Jim Harbaugh brought in **Mike McDaniel** as offensive coordinator to unlock Justin Herbert, and instead the Chargers are 0-2 with identical 26-14 home losses to Arizona and Las Vegas. Herbert is 32-of-54 for 401 yards with 2 TDs and 3 INTs, the offense sits 28th in points per drive at 1.22, and Los Angeles has run only 27% of its snaps in opponent territory, worst in the NFL.

#### Offence against defence

**Buffalo's run game and Allen's legs meet the one thing Los Angeles does well.** The Chargers are allowing just 86.5 rush yards per game, rank around 8th in EPA per rush allowed, and bottled up Ashton Jeanty in Week 2, so **James Cook III** (34 carries, 192 yards) may have to grind. That matters less at the goal line, where Allen is essentially a designed short-yardage runner and has already scored from one and two yards out this season.
- Buffalo has hit 20+ yard runs four times, tied for most in the NFL, and leads the league in play-action success at 62%.

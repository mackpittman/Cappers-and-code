HOW IT WORKS

Your slip,
straight to your book.

Build it once in the app. One paste in here and every leg is loaded, ready to place.

1

Tap + Slip on any pick

Anywhere in the app. Sides, totals, scorers, whole tickets. They all land on today's slip.

2

Open My Slip

Everything you tapped is already selected. Untick anything you are not betting. The count and the parlayed price update as you go.

3

Hit Send to Gambly

Top of the page. It copies your picks and brings you straight here. SEND 5 TO GAMBLY

4

Paste it and send

Long press the box, paste, send. The tag is already in the text, so do not retype anything.

5

Tap your book

GamblyBot replies with a one-click betslip. Pick your book at the top of it and every leg is loaded. The reply shows on the Feed tab in the app too.

CHECK THE NUMBER

Gambly loads whatever line is live right now, which may not be the number on your slip. The app is the price of record. Look at every leg before you place it.
